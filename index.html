About Me

Who I Am

I am a Rwandan Statistician, Data Analyst, and Data Visualization Specialist with a BSc in Applied Statistics and Demography from the University of Rwanda (Class of 2024). I specialize in transforming raw survey and administrative data into clear, evidence-based insight — through statistical analysis, interactive dashboards, and structured reporting.

The problems I enjoy solving sit at the intersection of data quality and decision-making: cleaning messy survey data, designing indicators that actually answer the question being asked, and building dashboards that let non-technical stakeholders explore evidence themselves instead of waiting on a static report.

I'm especially drawn to public health, official statistics, monitoring & evaluation, and economic data — sectors where good analysis has a direct line to better decisions for real people, whether that's a ministry tracking a national indicator or an NGO reporting on programme impact.

Rwanda DHS 2019–20 Health Intelligence Dashboard

Python Streamlit Plotly GeoPandas Pandas

Interactive public health dashboard developed from DHS microdata covering more than 35,000 survey records. Features geospatial analysis, demographic indicators, maternal health, fertility, nutrition, and province-level insights.
